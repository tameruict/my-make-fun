1 COURTS · GLOBAL TECH

US JURY CONVICTS HUAWEI ON 10 OF 12 FRAUD, SANCTIONS & TRADE-SECRET COUNTS

A federal jury in Brooklyn (Tue in New York, Wed in Asia) convicted Huawei Technologies on 10 of 12 felony counts—bank and wire fraud, Iran sanctions violations, and trade-secret theft conspiracy—after a 12-week trial; sentencing is 14 Sep 2027. (Reuters / DOJ / SCMP)

2 CRISIS · COLOMBIA

GUNMEN HOLD 25 HOSTAGES IN CARTAGENA SUPERMARKET SIEGE AFTER ARMOURED-TRUCK RAID

Robbers fleeing a botched armoured-truck raid seized ~30 hostages inside a Cartagena Éxito supermarket (Tue afternoon in Cartagena — Wed in Asia). Snipers sealed the area; 10 hostages were freed by 00:57 UTC Wednesday as the siege continued. (AP / AFP / Sky)

3 CONFLICT · UKRAINE WAR

NEW RFE/RL ANALYSIS ESTIMATES OVER 700,000 RUSSIAN SOLDIERS KILLED IN UKRAINE WAR

A new analysis published Wednesday at 01:00 UTC by Radio Free Europe/Radio Liberty estimates that more than 700,000 Russian soldiers have been killed since the full-scale invasion of Ukraine began in February 2022. (RFE/RL)

4 ECONOMY · AUSTRALIA

AUSTRALIA OCTOBER CPI JUMPS TO 4.9% Y/Y AS ELECTRICITY COSTS SURGE +25.2%

Australian Bureau of Statistics data released Wednesday at 00:30 GMT showed October CPI jumping to 4.9% y/y from 4.3% in September (above 4.8% forecast) and trimmed mean rising to 3.8%, driven by electricity +25.2%, transport +10.6%, and housing +6.4%, putting a December RBA hike above 4.85% back in play. (ABS / WSJ / FXStreet)

5 MARKETS · ASIA-PACIFIC

9 HUMAN RIGHTS · UN / AFRICA

UN OPENS 16 DAYS CAMPAIGN AGAINST GENDER VIOLENCE WEDNESDAY; 47,000 KILLED IN 2025

(UN Women / UNODC)
