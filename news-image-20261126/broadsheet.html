1DISASTER · EGYPT

FUEL TANKER INFERNO KILLS AT LEAST 31 ON GREATER CAIRO RING ROAD

A fuel tanker veered off, overturned and exploded above Tunnel 10 at Al-Warraq, Giza, on Wednesday evening (Thursday in Asia); fire engulfed 27 vehicles. Health Ministry: at least 31 killed, 21 hurt; 39 ambulances sent. (AP / CNN / Al Jazeera)

2TRADE · WASHINGTON

TRUMP PROPOSES SECTION 301 TARIFFS ON 14 PARTNERS — 20% ON CHINA

President Trump proposed country-specific Section 301 tariffs Wednesday morning in Washington (Thursday in Asia) after an 8-month USTR probe: 20% on China atop ~25%, 15% caps for the EU, Japan, Korea, Taiwan and Switzerland; comments due Jan 5. (USTR / Reuters)

3ECONOMY · WASHINGTON

US ECONOMY GREW 4.3% IN Q3; INFLATION 3.8% AS FED HIKE BETS NEAR 97%

The US economy grew an annualised 4.3% in Q3 (revised up from 3.4%), the BEA reported Wednesday (Thursday in Sydney); October PCE inflation was 3.8% year on year, core 3.3%. Markets now price a ~97% chance of a December Fed hike. (BEA / Reuters)

4MARKETS · NY & SF

WALL STREET WILD COMEBACK AS NASDAQ CUTS LOSSES TO 0.6%

The Nasdaq closed down 0.62% Wednesday after plunging 4.6% intraday; the Dow rose 0.33% after a 1,100-point plunge and Nvidia fell 3.9% after a 9% slide. The 10-year yield held at 4.85%. (AP / WSJ)

5CRISIS · COLOMBIA

POLICE REVEAL 76 HOSTAGES HELD IN CARTAGENA SUPERMARKET SIEGE

Colombian police revealed late Wednesday (Thursday in Sydney) that 76 people were held in the Cartagena supermarket siege — more than double the ~30 first reported. All were freed before dawn Wednesday; one assailant was killed. (AFP / Arab News)

6DIPLOMACY · FLORIDA

TRUMP HOSTS NATO CHIEF RUTTE FOR CLOSED-DOOR DINNER AT MAR-A-LAGO

Trump hosts NATO chief Mark Rutte for a closed-door dinner at Mar-a-Lago on Wednesday evening (Thursday in Sydney), with no joint readout planned; ahead of the dinner, Rutte told Fox News Trump is 'testing Putin'. (Palm Beach Post / Fox / NATO)

7CONFLICT · SAUDI ARABIA

SAUDI DEFENCES INTERCEPT HOUTHI MISSILES OVER RIYADH

Saudi defences intercepted two Houthi ballistic missiles over Riyadh early Thursday, after a Houthi strike on Jazan port and a drone intercept near Jeddah Islamic Port on Wednesday. Secretary Rubio condemned the strikes. (Saudi-led coalition / Arab News)

8WEATHER · USA

THANKSGIVING STORM FORCES HISTORIC FIRST MACY'S PARADE POSTPONEMENT

A Thanksgiving Day nor'easter battered the East Coast on Thursday, snarling holiday travel and forcing the first-ever postponement of the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade, to Saturday 28 November. US markets are closed Thursday. (AP / CBS / Axios)

9OBSERVANCE · INDIA

INDIA MARKS CONSTITUTION DAY AS SAMVIDHAN DIVAS

India marks Constitution Day (Samvidhan Divas) on Thursday 26 November, commemorating the adoption of the Constitution in 1949 and honouring Dr B.R. Ambedkar's drafting legacy. (selfstudys / Indian Govt)

10BUSINESS · LONDON

POUNDLAND FILES FOR ADMINISTRATION; 600 STORES AND 12,000 JOBS AT RISK

Poundland filed for administration on Wednesday, Sky News reported, with AlixPartners appointed; more than 600 stores and about 10,000–12,000 jobs are at risk across the UK and Ireland. (Sky News / Reuters)

11TECH · WASHINGTON

SENATOR BOOKER DEMANDS ANSWERS FROM NVIDIA OVER HUANG'S RETURN

Senator Cory Booker, the top Democrat on the Senate antitrust subcommittee, demanded answers from Nvidia on Wednesday about CEO Jensen Huang's return, pressing the Trump administration's role and the board's probe of Huang's China links. (Reuters)

12CRIME · NIGERIA

FIVE MEXICANS ARRESTED IN NIGERIA OVER $227M INDUSTRIAL METH LAB

Five Mexican nationals were arrested in Nigeria after the NDLEA dismantled a massive industrial methamphetamine lab in Enugu State, seizing 1.5 tonnes of meth and chemicals valued at $227 million. (BBC Africa / NDLEA)

13SPORT · GENEVA

GUKESH AND SINDAROV TIED 1–1 AS WORLD CHESS GAME 3 BEGINS IN GENEVA

Gukesh Dommaraju and Javokhir Sindarov are tied 1.0–1.0 after Game 2 of the FIDE World Chess Championship in Geneva; Game 3 begins Thursday 26 November at 15:00 CET. (FIDE / Chess.com / Guardian)
